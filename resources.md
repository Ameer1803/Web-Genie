4. Vite & Next.js & CRA
5. Node.js & pure javascript coding
6. Simple backend via Supabase (Auth & SQL)
7. CORS (Simple read, but important)
8. Caching(local \& server) and CDNs
9. What exactly is an API? What is REST API? What other types are there
